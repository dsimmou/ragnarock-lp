Common Questions

Is Ragnarok: The New World free?

Yes. The game is free to download and play on Steam. Optional in-game purchases are available.

Do I need a Gravity account?

Yes. A free account is required to play. The game asks you to create one when prompted on first launch.

I've never played an MMORPG. Is it beginner-friendly?

Yes. The game is free, you can switch between 8 classes anytime on the same character, and you can team up with friends in parties of 5 while you learn.

What are the PC requirements?

Windows 10 or Windows 11 (no macOS or Linux version), 12 GB of RAM minimum, 30 GB of storage and a broadband internet connection.
